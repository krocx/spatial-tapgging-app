One direction of trustThe envelope proves what (hashes, signature); the store holds everything; the bundle says how to run it. A reader can verify a .tag with no network, but cannot obtain a single byte of content without an authorised channel.
Same frame, three placesanchorPose in the envelope, the sealed map's meta in the store, and anchor.frames in the bundle are the same origin written three ways - so a pin placed on the iPad lands on the headset within millimetres.
What a change doesEdit a step in the Designer → the store changes → the chamber's contentVersion and its member hashes change → every cached envelope reads as stale → the next bundle carries the new deltas. Nothing is edited in place downstream.
