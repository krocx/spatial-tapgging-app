Colour

From the Applied team library. Focus blue and Warm green never carry text — they fail contrast on paper (2.2:1 and 2.3:1). They are fills, rings, marks and the wordmark; the -ink variants do the reading work. The system has no orange: warnings are deep blue ink with the mark, and one red is reserved for real failures.

The mark & the motion

Registration mark

Four brackets closing on a target: the QR finder square, the wafer alignment cross, the AR anchor. It is the focus ring, the loading state, the section marker and the wordmark's dot. <span class="ax-mark"><i></i><em></em></span>, sized with font-size.

Lock-in — the one motion

Elements register: scale 1.35 → 1 in 220 ms on the brand ease. Nothing fades in, nothing slides. Loading is the mark locking in. .ax-lockin on arrival, .ax-loading on a container. Reduced-motion collapses it to 1 ms.

Icons

One sprite, /portal/brand/icons.svg: the AppliedX icon library the Procedure Designer draws with plus the UI set that replaces every emoji. 24-grid, 1.5-px round strokes, currentColor. <svg class="ax-icon"><use href="/portal/brand/icons.svg#i-check"/></svg>

Rules — enforced by npm run brand:check

Always
  • Colours, sizes and durations from tokens.css only.
  • Icons from the sprite; text is text, icons are icons.
  • The mark for focus, loading and section markers; lock-in for arrival.
  • Roboto for reading, Roboto Mono for identifiers and numbers.
  • Product colour on chips, cards and diagram bars — the same colour on the map, the portal and the app.
  • Hairlines for elevation; 2-px corners.
Never
  • Glass blur, gradients on surfaces, drop shadows.
  • Emoji as icons. Rounded 12-px cards. Pill chips.
  • Orange, amber or yellow anywhere but the Gemba product mark.
  • Focus blue or Warm green as text.
  • Navy dashboards, electric-blue accents, system-blue links.
  • A page-level palette or font — if it is not in brand/, it does not exist.
