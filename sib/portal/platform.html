Scan the chamber once and every guide and spatial tag knows exactly where it lives - every session, every shift, every technician. If the part moves, the instruction moves with it: a thirty-second scan lets SIB know a chamber by its shape.

3 · Spatial validation

SIB checks the work, not the checkbox.

A cone of light brings the camera to the exact spot the ME trained. The verdict is on the part, with the evidence attached to the run - the QFE reviews by exception.

In the catalogue
4 · Presence & coaching

The ME and the technician, on the same pin.

The ME sees which step the technician is on and where they are looking, and sends a "look here" that lands on the metal. New hires learn on the tool, not in a classroom. Hints arrive only when a visit differs from what is normal on that step.

Responsible by design: presence is per session, announced, never recorded.

6 · From the 3D OMS to the AR OMS

The content you already have becomes the instruction on the tool.

A 3D OMS publication - every part, every motion, every view - imports in one step and comes out as an AR work instruction. Nothing is re-authored. The ME adjusts it in the 3D Studio: find a part, colour it, hide it, say what the operator sees. Excel, JSON and the Designer are the other lanes today; live pull from MES is the destination.

7 · Next-generation XR · device readiness

One instruction. Every device. Measured, not assumed.

The same guide runs on the iPad, in the browser of a Quest, HoloLens or Vision Pro through the XR kit, reduced on waveguide glasses, as text on Even G2 and RealWear, and read aloud on Oakley. SIB derives what each step needs and says, per device, whether it is delivered as authored, adapted, or not at all - before any hardware is bought.

8 · Why SIB leads

The spatial intelligence is ours. The hardware is a choice.

Every piece that matters is written by Applied Materials: the importer, the reducer that fits content to a device, the backbone that gives a chamber one origin, the validation that checks the work, the kits that deliver it. When the headset changes - and it will - the origins, pins, validations, run history and learnings stay where they are.

Protected by design: the spatial data formats and validation methods are patent-pending and never leave the company's servers.
