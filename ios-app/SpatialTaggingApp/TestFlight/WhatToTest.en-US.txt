Spatial Tagging for AR OMS · 2026.4.50

NEW IN THIS BUILD

Readings, labels and barcodes on a step. A step can now check what the camera reads: a value on an instrument display (multimeter, torque tool, leak checker), words on a label, or a barcode / QR / Data Matrix. The frame with the read is the step's evidence.

• Label checks mean "must say", not "says only" - a real label carries more than the words that matter.
• Barcodes: 1D, QR, Data Matrix, PDF417 and Aztec are decoded on the same frame as the text; several in frame are listed by kind.
• Instrument displays: a reading is sampled three times over a second and the stable value kept; a display still settling says so. Scientific notation as leak checkers print it (1.2×10⁻⁹, 1.2E-09, mantissa and exponent on separate lines).
• One validation on the floor: when presence validates in the cone, the label or display is read on that same frame; both pass in one go when it is legible from there, otherwise the reading sheet opens with presence kept.
• Author mode: reading the real label in Place Steps in AR sets the words the way the camera spells them (tap the line); "Try it as the operator" on any trained step shows the operator's verdict without a save, publish or mode switch.
• Labels in Korean, Japanese and Chinese: the reader recognises Hangul, kana, kanji and both Chinese scripts, and says which language the line is in ("Label says "注意" - found · Japanese").
• SIB: what the camera read shows on every step visit in the Usage Log, not only on completed runs.
